# User guide

## First use

The app initializes a local profile and five level-1 attributes. Edit your name, role, monthly income, and currency under **System**. Income is manually entered and used only to label suggestion affordability; recommendations are not purchases.

## Daily quests

The app generates up to three short missions for the local day when opened, unless recovery mode is enabled or today's missions already exist. Review the evidence prompt, report what you actually did, and mark the quest complete. Reports are self-reported and no GPS, health, or independent verification is used. A quest that passes its deadline cannot award XP.

## Skills

Add a skill by name and optionally check existing prerequisite skills. A skill with missing/under-level prerequisites is locked. Edit requirements on its card; cycles and nonexistent dependencies are rejected. Linked quest practice earns that skill's independent XP. Archive hides a skill from active use but preserves it.

## Rewards and challenges

Complete every required daily quest to unlock the personal purchase and leisure-exploration claims. Each can be claimed once in a day; spending is optional. Start at most one dungeon. Completed quests advance it. Ending a dungeon does not delete its history.

## Recovery and missed quests

Enable recovery mode under System to pause new quests. This does not erase progress. Missed quests expire and may produce a gentle, optional recovery follow-up after the 24-hour grace period. Do not train through pain or illness; skip or pause when necessary.

## Backups

Use **Export JSON** in System to download a backup. **Import backup** replaces current app data after an explicit confirmation. **Clear all data** also requires confirmation; it cannot be undone. Browser data is not synchronized across devices.
